The Name

Why 10N?

10N refers to Latitude 10° North — the line that runs through the heart of Southeast Asia, crossing Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, and the Philippines. It's the geography this business was built to serve.

It's also a play on a perfect score of 10 using the initial of the principal consultant — Neeraj Dhanothia. Two meanings, one name: regional roots, and a commitment to excellence.

How We Think

Principles that guide every engagement

Diagnose before prescribing

📐

Systems over heroics

Good operations shouldn't depend on any one person. We build systems, processes, and infrastructure that work without constant intervention.

📊

Measure everything

Improvements without measurement are just stories. We define success metrics before we start and report against them honestly.

🤝

Hands-on delivery

We don't hand you a slide deck and walk away. We work alongside your team to implement, not just advise.
