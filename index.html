Methodology

Lean Six Sigma · DMAIC

Every process improvement engagement follows the proven DMAIC framework — Define, Measure, Analyse, Improve, Control. This ensures improvements are evidence-based, measurable, and sustainable.

  • Define: scope the problem with precision
  • Measure: baseline current performance
  • Analyse: identify verified root causes
  • Improve: design and implement solutions
  • Control: lock in gains and prevent regression
